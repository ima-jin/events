# 05 - Kernel gaps

Every `gap(kernel)` the design depends on, written so each can be filed as a kernel issue. The kernel repo is not touched by this work. Ids `Gnn` are cited from `02` and `03`; `Dnn` are the decision cards that make a gap conditional. Evidence paths are in `ima-jin/imajin-ai` at `main` e878ce27.

Each entry: what the app needs, what exists today, what it blocks.

## A. Capabilities an app cannot reach today

- `gap(kernel)` G01 - Delegated event DID registration. Need: a registered app can register a sub-identity (an event DID) as a delegate of the app's own key, so events does not generate and store a private key per event. Today: `POST auth /api/register` requires a payload signed by the new identity's own key (`apps/events/app/api/events/route.ts` signs it with a freshly generated key). Blocks: D09 option b. Conditional: not needed if D09 option a or c is chosen.
- `gap(kernel)` G03 - App-authenticated notification send, with per-scope user preferences for app-owned scopes. Need: an app can send a notification to a DID under its own app token, naming a scope (for example `event:ticket-confirmed`) and supplying the content, and the kernel applies the recipient's opt-out for that scope and handles delivery; apps can register their scopes so they appear on the user's notification settings pages. Today: `notify /api/send` and `/api/broadcast` accept only a shared `x-webhook-secret`; templates and scopes are kernel code (`apps/kernel/src/lib/notify/templates.ts`, settings pages list `event:ticket` and `event:registration`). Blocks: the confirmation verb (D02), organizer messaging, R28.
- `gap(kernel)` G04 - App-callable identity operations without an internal key. Need: (a) tier eligibility evaluation for a DID after check-in; (b) a way to read a DID's verified contact email with the user's consent or an explicit scope; (c) profile resolve (handle, display name) for batches of DIDs by app token. Today: `auth /api/eligibility/evaluate` and `auth /api/identity/{did}/contact` require the kernel internal API key; `profile /api/resolve` accepts a bearer or internal key; `@imajin/auth` helpers (`resolveEmailForDid`, `resolveIdentitiesForDids`) use kernel-internal credentials. Blocks: K04, K05, K06; the Identity port.
- `gap(kernel)` G05 - App-scoped pay rights. Need: scopes (for example `pay:refund`, `pay:charge-pledges`) grantable to a registered app, so refunds and pledge charges are authorized by the app token and limited to sessions the app created. Also confirm whether `POST pay /api/balance/transfer` and `POST pay /api/checkout` are guarded at all. Today: `pay /api/refund`, `/api/charge-pledges` and `/api/settle` require `Bearer PAY_SERVICE_API_KEY` (a shared service key held in events' environment); `balance/transfer` and `checkout` show no guard identifier in the route file. Blocks: D06, R08, R41, R57, R12.
- `gap(kernel)` G06 - Pay read model for apps. Need: read a checkout session or order's settlement detail (gross, fees, net, chain of payees, tax) by the app's own session or order id, with the app token. Today: events reads `pay.transactions` directly in the sales route and receives settlement by webhook. Blocks: R36, R37, the D04 cache.
- `gap(kernel)` G07 - Claim signal for guest tickets. Need: when a soft identity becomes a hard one, the app can learn it safely, either through the session carrying a verified email claim the app can use at login, or through a signed `identity.claimed` delivery to subscribed apps. Today: `auth/onboard/verify` fires an unauthenticated call to `events /events/api/migrate-tickets` with the email and new DID. Blocks: D10, R55 removal, K05.
- `gap(kernel)` G08 - App-callable pod membership. Need: list members of a pod by pod id and add or remove a member, by app token. Today: `connections /api/pods` and `/api/pods/{id}` accept a session only. Blocks: the one-time import of existing co-hosts into `event_organizers` (D07) and the deferred pod mirror.
- `gap(kernel)` G09 - App-callable chat operations. Need: ensure an event conversation exists for an event DID, add and remove members, and set conversation context (title, image, start time), by app token. Today: `chat /api/d/{did}/members` accepts a session; `/api/d/{did}/context` accepts the internal key or a session; `chat /api/participants/migrate` shows no guard. Blocks: the lobby (K12), D18.
- `gap(kernel)` G13 - Platform-admin scope for apps. Need: a grantable scope or claim that lets an app token act as platform admin for the admin-only export (R02). Today: `requireAdmin` from `@imajin/auth`. Blocks: R02 only.
- `gap(kernel)` G14 - Browser access to the caller's connection list for the co-host picker. Need: `connections /api/connections` callable from the events origin with the user's session (CORS and cookie policy), or a batched search by handle. Today: it shows no guard identifier and events proxies it through R58. Blocks: R58 removal.
- `gap(kernel)` G16 - Payment-completion delivery to registered apps. Need: when a pay checkout session created by an app completes or fails, pay delivers a signed event to a callback URL stored on the app's registry entry, with retries and a documented signature scheme, plus a session-status read the app can poll for reconciliation. Today: pay's webhook handler calls `EVENTS_SERVICE_URL/api/webhook/payment` with `Bearer EVENTS_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, hard-wired by environment variable in `apps/kernel/src/lib/pay/webhook-handlers.ts` (a coffee-specific copy exists in `stripe-bus-consumer.ts`). Blocks: D05, R65.
- `gap(kernel)` G17 - App-issued attestations about a third-party subject. Need: clarity on, and if absent support for, an app issuing `ticket.purchased`, `event.created` and `event.attendance` attestations where the subject is a buyer, an organizer or an attendee, under the app's delegated authority. Today: the bus runs those attestations as reactors (`packages/bus/src/config.ts`); `POST auth /api/attestations` takes a bearer but Dykil's design has the respondent as issuer, and whether an app token may issue about someone else is not established. Blocks: the Records port, D03.
- `gap(kernel)` G18 - Guest claim link minted for an app. Need: an app can request a one-time claim or onboarding link for an email, to put in a ticket email, without the kernel sending its own email. Today: `createOnboardToken` in events inserted into `auth.onboard_tokens` directly; `POST auth /api/onboard` sends its own email and returns no token. Conditional: needed only if D11 option a is chosen.
- `gap(kernel)` G19 - Settlement started by pay from the checkout. Need: pay accepts a reference to the event's `.fair` manifest (or the manifest, signed by the organizer) when the app creates the session, validates it against the session, and runs settlement itself on completion, so events never publishes `order.completed`. Today: events publishes `order.completed` with `issuer` set to the buyer and the manifest in the payload, and the kernel `settle` reactor acts on it (`apps/events/app/api/webhook/payment/route.ts`, `packages/bus/src/config.ts`). Blocks: D04.

## B. Kernel code that must stop depending on events (prune prerequisites)

These are not missing features; they are places where the kernel reaches into events and must be changed before `apps/events` is removed (#2517). None of them is needed for the dev soak or the prod cutover to proceed, because the kernel can keep reading the shared `events` tables and calling the same URLs until the prune; none is to be done before the prod cutover sign-off.

- `gap(kernel)` G02 - Retire the hard-wired events coupling. Remove `notifyEventsService` and the `EVENTS_WEBHOOK_SECRET` use in `apps/kernel/src/lib/pay/webhook-handlers.ts`; remove the `settlement.completed` webhook reactor that posts to `EVENTS_SERVICE_URL/api/webhook/settlement` in `packages/bus/src/config.ts`; retire the bus chains for the 16 of the 17 events types that have one (code defaults and the seeded `kernel.bus_chain_configs` rows from migrations 0039, 0135 and 0170); remove `EVENTS_SERVICE_URL`, `EVENTS_WEBHOOK_SECRET` and `WEBHOOK_SECRET` use for events from setup and deploy scripts.
- `gap(kernel)` G10 - Presence tools and public reads. `packages/llm/src/tools/events.ts` (wired by the profile `query` and `stream` routes) calls events with the internal API key. Re-point to the public read with a scoped token, or drop the tool until app discovery exists.
- `gap(kernel)` G11 - Chat conversation naming. `apps/kernel/src/lib/chat/message-helpers.ts` fetches `events /api/events/by-did/{did}` to title an event lobby. Take the title from the conversation context that the app now sets (G09) and delete the call.
- `gap(kernel)` G12 - Chat access for event DIDs. `apps/kernel/src/lib/kernel/access.ts` and `apps/kernel/app/auth/api/access/[did]/route.ts` read `events.tickets` and `events.events` directly. Decide by chat membership alone, which the app maintains through G09. This is a hard blocker for the prune. It does not block the schema-scoped role for the events app (D12), which restricts what events can read, not what the kernel can read.
- `gap(kernel)` G15 - Registry-driven app links. Navigation (`NavBar`, `NavBarWithUnread`) and the profile community tabs use `NEXT_PUBLIC_EVENTS_URL` and `EVENTS_SERVICE_URL`. Read the registered app's URL from the registry instead.
- `gap(kernel)` G20 - Onboard-verify call. Delete the fire-and-forget `events /events/api/migrate-tickets` call in `apps/kernel/app/auth/api/onboard/verify/route.ts` once G07 exists.
- `gap(kernel)` G21 - Notify templates and bus notify reactors for `event:*`. After the templates move to the events repo (D02) remove the 7 `event:*` templates from `apps/kernel/src/lib/notify/templates.ts` and the bus reactors that use them, while keeping the scopes registered (G03) so users keep their opt-outs.
- `gap(kernel)` G22 - Kernel migrations and scripts. Stop carrying kernel migrations for the eight `events` tables (they are not kernel-owned; they are never dropped by the kernel), and remove `apps/events` entries from pm2 checks and `scripts/` (for example `scripts/check-pm2-status.test.sh`, `scripts/setup-local.sh`).

## C. Not kernel gaps, but to be filed alongside

- `gap(dykil)` Batched lookup of a survey's responses by ticket id (or by list of ticket ids). Dykil offers an owner export of all responses and a per-ticket existence check, but not "responses for these N tickets", which the guest list needs. Without it D08 option b is the only practical path (the projection), and the projection needs a repair path from the owner export.
- `gap(events)` in the kernel copy, found while mapping and live today: the ticket gate and "attending" list count only `sold` and `used` while paid tickets are `valid` (F01); `/migrate-tickets` is effectively unauthenticated (F03); `/upload` and `/register/[ticketId]` carry no guard (F04, F05). The design fixes these in the new app; whether to patch the kernel copy before cutover is a separate call, because patching it edits `apps/events` in the kernel, which this work does not do.
- `gap(events)` Template drift in `ima-jin/events`: the `/claim` route and page, `middleware.ts`, `docs/DEPLOY.md`, the registry-deps, migrations and audit scripts and their workflows are missing relative to `imajin-app-template`. Handled by the template sync in the #2518 re-cut.

## D. Which gaps block the prod cutover and which have a fallback

- Hard blocker with no fallback: G03 (without app-authenticated notification send, buyers get no confirmation).
- Time-boxed fallback available, accepted explicitly by Ryan: G16 (keep the hard-wired pay webhook and its bearer secret until the prune, D05 option c), G05 (keep the shared pay service key until the prune, D06 option b), G04 (eligibility and contact calls degrade to deferred retries; they are not on the request path), G17 (skip the purchase and attendance attestations until supported, which loses the signed records for the interim and must be called out), G09 (skip the chat lobby; it is not part of the outcome), G07 (keep the existing `/migrate-tickets` contract for the cutover, D10 option c; the kernel caller sends no credential and the kernel is not modified before cutover, so finding F03 stays open until G07 and G20 land, and Ryan must accept that).
- Not needed for cutover: G01 (D09 falls back to today's registration), G06, G08 (an operator export of pod members can seed `event_organizers`), G13, G14, G18, G19 (D04 option a keeps today's behaviour), and everything in section B.

## E. Summary by outcome verb

- Create an event: G01 (conditional), G09, G17.
- Sell tickets: G16, G05, G19, G06, G04 (guest identity read).
- Buyers get confirmations: G03 (blocking), G18 (conditional).
- Check people in: G04 (eligibility), G17; no gap on the request path.
- Prune: G02, G10, G11, G12, G15, G20, G21, G22.
